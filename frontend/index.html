How CleanRoute AI Works

Traditional GPS engines only optimize for speed or distance. CleanRoute AI adds an environmental intelligence layer to assist conscious commuter decisions.

1

Enter Destination

Input your origin, destination, and select your preferred travel mode and optimization priority.

2

Compare Route Options

Evaluate alternative paths across distance, estimated time, and route infrastructure.

3

Analyze Environmental Data

Incorporate real-time atmospheric indicators, particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10), and wind factors.

4

Get AI Recommendation

Receive an explainable recommendation balancing health exposure and practical travel convenience.
