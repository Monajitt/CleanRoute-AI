# Walking and Pedestrian Mobility

## The Value of Walking in Urban Mobility
Walking is the most fundamental, inclusive, and environmentally sustainable form of active transportation. It produces zero operational greenhouse gas emissions, generates no tailpipe pollutants, requires minimal physical infrastructure, and contributes significantly to cardiovascular fitness and mental well-being.

## The Exposure Duration Trade-Off
While walking is completely clean for the environment, pedestrians face a unique physiological exposure balance:
- **Travel Speed vs. Exposure Duration:** Because walking speed averages 4.5 to 5.5 km/h, pedestrians spend considerably longer traversing a given geographical distance than vehicular travelers.
- **Physical Infiltration:** Even though a pedestrian's respiratory rate is lower than a vigorous cyclist's, the extended duration spent in proximity to traffic corridors can increase total cumulative particulate inhalation if traveling along busy roads.
- **Micro-Environment Separation:** Selecting pedestrian walkways set back from curbs, walking through parks, or using tree-lined secondary streets drastically reduces the concentration of primary vehicle pollutants inhaled per kilometer.

## Walking Support in CleanRoute AI
CleanRoute AI models dedicated pedestrian pathways using OpenRouteService foot-walking networks. When the user selects Walking mode, CleanRoute AI prioritizes pedestrian-safe geometries, balancing travel duration with localized pollution estimates to suggest paths with lower estimated cumulative exposure.
