# Cycling and Active Urban Commuting

## The Role of Cycling in Sustainable Cities
Cycling offers an ideal balance between spatial speed and environmental sustainability. Covering distances of 3 to 15 kilometers at typical speeds of 15 to 22 km/h, bicycles produce zero direct exhaust emissions, relieve vehicular traffic congestion, reduce urban noise pollution, and provide substantial aerobic exercise.

## Ventilation Rates and Clean Corridor Importance
During active cycling, human respiration increases proportionally with cadence and gradient:
- **Elevated Inhalation Volume:** A cyclist's ventilation rate frequently increases by a factor of 2.5x to 4x compared to resting state, resulting in a higher volume of air entering deep lung tissue every minute.
- **Physical Proximity:** When riding in mixed traffic without dedicated physical barriers, cyclists inhale vehicle exhaust plumes before they have dispersed into the ambient atmosphere.
- **Route Deflection Advantage:** Modest geographical route deviations (e.g., opting for a greenway, canal path, or residential cycle boulevard instead of a multi-lane arterial road) often add only 1 to 3 minutes of journey time while substantially lowering estimated PM2.5 and NO2 exposure.
