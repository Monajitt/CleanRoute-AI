# Responsible AI Principles and System Limitations

## Transparency and Fairness
CleanRoute AI adheres to principles of responsible artificial intelligence, prioritizing transparent decision logic, explainability, user privacy, and strict avoidance of misleading health guarantees.

## Informational Nature and Non-Medical Boundaries
1. **No Medical Advice:** CleanRoute AI is not a healthcare diagnostic or therapeutic platform. The environmental and air quality metrics presented are purely informational indicators intended to assist comparative travel choices.
2. **No Health Risk Certainty:** Atmospheric values reflect modeled sensor feeds and numerical interpolation. CleanRoute AI never describes any route as "100% safe," "medically risk-free," or "guaranteed healthy."
3. **Approved Terminology:** The system consistently utilizes calibrated, non-prescriptive language:
   - *"Recommended Route"*
   - *"Lower Estimated Pollution Exposure"*
   - *"Pollution-Aware Route Recommendation"*
   - *"Estimated Air Quality Conditions"*

## Modeling Uncertainties and Environmental Dynamics
- **Sensor Grid Resolution:** Regional air quality data originates from satellite observations, ground monitoring stations, and numerical dispersion models (e.g., Open-Meteo Air Quality). Micro-scale concentrations (such as a smoker on a sidewalk or temporary idling trucks) may vary from interpolated grid averages.
- **Dynamic Meteorology:** Weather, wind shifts, and sudden traffic incidents can alter localized pollution rapidly.
- **Subjective Preferences:** Preferences (Health First, Balanced, Time First) represent mathematically weighted trade-offs, not universal absolutes.

## Privacy and Data Minimization
CleanRoute AI operates on principles of privacy by design:
- User route searches require no personal user registration, demographic profiling, or medical history disclosure.
- Geocoding and routing queries are processed without retaining personal identifiers.
